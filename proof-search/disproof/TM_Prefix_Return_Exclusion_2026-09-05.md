# Every positively realized fixed Thue–Morse valuation morphism starts on a cycle

**Mathematical scope:** an analytic auxiliary theorem about structured valuation codes. No universal Collatz convergence or exclusion of all nontrivial positive cycles is claimed. Novelty has not been established.

**Map convention:** throughout,

$$
U(n)=\frac{3n+1}{2^{v_2(3n+1)}}
$$

on positive odd integers. Every time index below counts fully accelerated odd steps. The coordinate maps, the one-division shortcut map, and the repository's normalized hard-return map are not substituted for this orbit.

## 1. General theorem and the repository consequence

Let $t=t_0t_1\cdots$ be the binary Thue–Morse word starting at $0$, generated by

$$
\mu(0)=01,\qquad \mu(1)=10.
$$

Choose **any two fixed nonempty finite words** $B_0,B_1$ of positive integers. Concatenate them in Thue–Morse order:

$$
\boldsymbol a=B_{t_0}B_{t_1}B_{t_2}\cdots.
$$

**Theorem.** If $\boldsymbol a$ is the exact valuation sequence of one positive odd integer $N$, then $N$ itself lies on a periodic odd Collatz orbit: $U^j(N)=N$ for some $j>0$.

In particular, no divergent positive orbit can use any such fixed binary Thue–Morse coding. Neither an average-valuation assumption nor a claim that the encoded word is aperiodic is needed.

Two consequences below exclude positive realization altogether:

1. the original code $a_i=1+t_i$, resolving the specific older 2-adic anchor's positive-integer membership gate negatively;
2. every fixed pair $B_0=1^p3$, $B_1=1^q3$, with $p,q\ge3$, including $p=q$.

The general theorem retains a necessary cycle exception. For example, $B_0=B_1=(2)$ is realized by $N=1$.

## 2. Exact finite-word collision

Let $w=(a_0,\ldots,a_{L-1})$, with $a_i\ge1$, and let $S=\sum_{i<L}a_i$. Two positive odd starting integers with these same next exact valuations satisfy

$$
x\equiv y\pmod {2^{S+1}}. \tag{1}
$$

For completeness, put $S_0=0$, $S_j=\sum_{i<j}a_i$, and

$$
C_w=\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}3^{L-1-j}2^{S_j}.
$$

The exact word gives

$$
3^Lx+C_w=2^S U^L(x).
$$

The endpoint is odd. Thus $3^Lx+C_w\equiv2^S\pmod {2^{S+1}}$. Subtracting the corresponding congruence for $y$, and using that $3^L$ is odd, proves (1). The endpoint oddness is the source of the extra $+1$.

Now write $n_j=U^j(N)$. Every accelerated step obeys

$$
U(x)+1\le\frac32(x+1),
\qquad
n_J+1\le\left(\frac32\right)^J(N+1). \tag{2}
$$

Suppose the initial valuation word of length $L$ occurs again at time $J$. If $n_J\ne N$, then (1) and positivity give

$$
2^{S+1}\le |n_J-N|
<\left(\frac32\right)^J(N+1). \tag{3}
$$

The strict upper bound also covers $n_J<N$, since the difference is smaller than the larger positive state plus one.

In the important case $L=2d$, $J=3d$, use $S\ge L$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{2\cdot32^d<27^d(N+1).} \tag{4}
$$

This is a bound for a **single fixed seed**, not a forbidden finite-word assertion.

### An effective linear consequence

Bernoulli's inequality gives

$$
\left(\frac{32}{27}\right)^d
=\left(1+\frac5{27}\right)^d
\ge1+\frac{5d}{27}.
$$

Combining it with (4) proves

$$
\boxed{10d+27<27N.} \tag{5}
$$

For natural integers this also yields

$$
d\le\left\lfloor\frac{27N-28}{10}\right\rfloor.
$$

Thus a return configuration with sufficiently large $d$ must have $n_{3d}=N$, rather than a nonzero collision gap.

## 3. Balanced Thue–Morse prefix returns

The word starts $01101001\cdots$. Its first two letters are $01$, and its letters at zero-based positions $3,4$ are also $01$. Applying $\mu^m$, with $h=2^m$, gives the exact identity

$$
t[0:2h]=t[3h:5h]. \tag{6}
$$

For $m\ge1$, each word $\mu^m(0)$ and $\mu^m(1)$ has $h/2$ copies of each letter. Consequently:

- the initial $2h$ letters contain $h$ copies of each letter;
- the initial $3h$ letters contain $3h/2$ copies of each letter.

Write $\ell_0=|B_0|$, $\ell_1=|B_1|$. Encoding (6) by the two fixed words gives equality of valuation words whose length and second starting time are exactly

$$
L=h(\ell_0+\ell_1)=2d,
\qquad
J=\frac{3h}{2}(\ell_0+\ell_1)=3d,
$$

where

$$
d=\frac h2(\ell_0+\ell_1).
$$

These are accelerated valuation lengths, even when the two codewords have different lengths. Balance makes the ratio exact. All quantities are integers because $m\ge1$, and $d$ grows without bound because the codewords are nonempty.

If the encoded word is positively realized, take $m$ sufficiently large that (5) fails. The collision theorem forces $n_{3d}=N$. Since $3d>0$, this proves the general theorem.

No recurrence-density or subword-complexity hypothesis was used. The conditional complexity theorem in the blind notes is a separate result and is not a dependency here.

## 4. Exact identity of the older quarantined anchor

The source checked is [`C-TM-MAHLER-ANCHOR-001` at immutable PR6 head `215f8e6ca0afae71f9e743ea683cc7263079f24c`](https://github.com/Sodelin/Collatz-Conjecture-Work/blob/215f8e6ca0afae71f9e743ea683cc7263079f24c/proof-search/disproof/CODEX_TM_MAHLER_ANCHOR_2026-08-24.md). That file explicitly uses the fully accelerated odd map, the unshifted bits $t_i=s_2(i)\bmod2$, and

$$
a_i=1+t_i,
\qquad
A_j=\sum_{i<j}a_i,
\qquad
N_{\rm TM}=-\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{2^{A_j}}{3^{j+1}}
\quad\text{in }\mathbb Z_2.
$$

Its product form is

$$
N_{\rm TM}=\frac19\prod_{m\ge0}
\left(1-\left(\frac89\right)^{2^m}\right)-6,
$$

interpreted 2-adically. No shift, complement, alternate coordinate, or different code is being substituted.

Suppose $N_{\rm TM}$ were a positive ordinary integer. The source's exact recurrence would make its odd orbit have precisely this valuation code. Apply the theorem with $B_0=(1)$, $B_1=(2)$. At the selected return times $J=3h$, $h=2^m$, $m\ge1$, the prefix is balanced, so its valuation sum is $3J/2$. The positive affine correction gives

$$
n_J>\frac{3^J}{2^{3J/2}}N_{\rm TM}>N_{\rm TM}.
$$

Thus the required cycle return is impossible. Therefore

$$
\boxed{N_{\rm TM}\notin\mathbb Z_{>0}.}
$$

The old product identity and conditional divergence implication remain intact. Their positive-membership antecedent has been excluded. This closes that particular disproof candidate; it is not a disproof of Collatz.

## 5. Fixed guarded hard-block morphisms

Fix $p,q\ge3$, and take

$$
B_0=1^p3,\qquad B_1=1^q3.
$$

Here $1^p3$ denotes $p$ valuations equal to $1$, followed by one valuation equal to $3$. Whenever the exact word $1^r3$ is realized from a positive odd $n$,

$$
U^{r+1}(n)
=\frac{3^{r+1}(n+1)-2^{r+1}}{2^{r+3}}. \tag{7}
$$

Subtracting $n$ produces numerator

$$
(3^{r+1}-2^{r+3})n+3^{r+1}-2^{r+1}.
$$

For every $r\ge3$, this is positive. Hence each complete block strictly increases the starting integer. Every selected return time $J=3d$ is a complete-block boundary, so $n_J>N$. The general theorem therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\text{No positive odd integer realizes the Thue–Morse concatenation of }1^p3,1^q3
\text{ for any fixed }p,q\ge3.}
$$

All integrality, parity, and exact-valuation guards occur in the premise that these are actual valuations. Equation (7) is not applied as an unguarded integer map.

### Guard and map comparison with the older endpoint route

The older [`F_bounded_alphabet_endpoint_residue_gate.md`, §7, at the same immutable PR6 head](https://github.com/Sodelin/Collatz-Conjecture-Work/blob/215f8e6ca0afae71f9e743ea683cc7263079f24c/proof-search/routes/F_bounded_alphabet_endpoint_residue_gate.md) writes post-first-step states of a $\{1,3\}$ code as $x=6z-1$. Its exact branches are

$$
a=1:\ z\mapsto3z/2\quad(z\text{ even}),
$$

$$
a=3:\ z\mapsto(3z+1)/8\quad(z\equiv5\pmod8).
$$

For $1^r3$, writing $z=2^r u$ makes the terminal guard $3^r u\equiv5\pmod8$, and the terminal coordinate is $(3^{r+1}u+1)/8$. This agrees with (7). The proof counts individual odd-map valuations, not coordinate updates or normalized hard-return edges.

The lower bound $r\ge3$ matters for the strict-growth argument. At $r=2$, the guarded counterexample is

$$
55\xrightarrow{a=1}83\xrightarrow{a=1}125\xrightarrow{a=3}47<55.
$$

The general Thue–Morse theorem still applies to any codewords containing such blocks; what fails at $r=2$ is this particular way of excluding the cycle exception.

## 6. Endpoint-carry consequences and remaining scope

Apply the retained PR6 bounded-alphabet endpoint theorem with its own canonical representatives $M_k$ and carries. For the excluded original anchor, whose valuation alphabet is $\{1,2\}$, it implies infinitely many positive carries and

$$
\limsup\frac{M_k}{3^k}\ge\frac14,
\qquad
\limsup M_k^{1/k}=3.
$$

For each excluded fixed hard morphism, the alphabet is $\{1,3\}$, so the corresponding consequences are

$$
\limsup\frac{M_k}{3^k}\ge\frac18,
\qquad
\limsup M_k^{1/k}=3.
$$

These are corollaries of that analytic endpoint theorem, with its exact definitions; they are not independently formalized here.

Every finite valuation word remains realizable by infinitely many positive starts. Arbitrary infinite hard-length sequences may fail the repeated-prefix hypothesis used here. No theorem makes all possible nonterminating valuation codes Thue–Morse morphisms, forces these long prefix returns, or excludes all unknown positive cycles. Global Collatz termination remains unresolved.
