# L1 — Exact affine prefix formula and non-descent upper bound

**Status:** `PROVED_AUX` by direct algebra; Lean formalization pending.

**Correctness:** high-confidence  
**Priority:** classical/folklore affine valuation-word machinery  
**Usefulness:** technically useful  
**Collatz relevance:** necessary condition for a minimal counterexample

## 1. Set-up

Let a positive odd accelerated Collatz trajectory be

$$
n_{j+1}=\frac{3n_j+1}{2^{a_j}},
\qquad
a_j=\nu_2(3n_j+1)\ge1.
$$

For `t>=0`, put

$$
A_t=\sum_{j=0}^{t-1}a_j,
\qquad A_0=0.
$$

Define

$$
C_t=\sum_{j=0}^{t-1}3^{t-1-j}2^{A_j}.
$$

Thus `C_0=0`, `C_1=1`, and

$$
C_{t+1}=3C_t+2^{A_t}.
$$

## 2. Exact affine-prefix identity

For every `t>=0`,

$$
\boxed{
n_t=\frac{3^t n_0+C_t}{2^{A_t}}.
}
$$

### Proof

The case `t=0` is immediate. If the identity holds at `t`, then

$$
\begin{aligned}
n_{t+1}
&=\frac{3n_t+1}{2^{a_t}}\\
&=\frac{3(3^t n_0+C_t)+2^{A_t}}{2^{A_t+a_t}}\\
&=\frac{3^{t+1}n_0+(3C_t+2^{A_t})}{2^{A_{t+1}}},
\end{aligned}
$$

which is the claimed formula with `C_{t+1}`.

## 3. Exact upper bound forced by failure to descend

Suppose `n_t >= n_0`. Then

$$
3^t n_0+C_t\ge2^{A_t}n_0.
$$

Therefore, whenever

$$
2^{A_t}>3^t,
$$

one has

$$
\boxed{
n_0\le
\frac{C_t}{2^{A_t}-3^t}.
}
$$

Equivalently, if

$$
n_0>
\frac{C_t}{2^{A_t}-3^t},
$$

then the trajectory **must** have descended by that particular endpoint:

$$
n_t<n_0.
$$

This is finite and exact; no asymptotic approximation is used.

## 4. Minimal-counterexample consequence

If `n_*` is the least positive odd counterexample from L0, then `n_t>=n_*` for every `t`. Consequently every prefix of its valuation sequence satisfying `2^{A_t}>3^t` must also satisfy

$$
\boxed{
n_*\le
B(a_0,\ldots,a_{t-1})
:=
\frac{C_t}{2^{A_t}-3^t}.
}
$$

Thus each sufficiently contracting valuation prefix gives an **explicit numerical ceiling** on any minimal counterexample realizing it.

Combined with an external verified lower bound `n_*>M`, such a prefix is impossible whenever

$$
B(a_0,\ldots,a_{t-1})\le M.
$$

The published computational verification through at least `2^71` therefore can be used as a legitimate base-case eliminator in a proof architecture, provided the external computation is imported as an explicitly labeled theorem/certificate rather than silently assumed.

## 5. Search interpretation

The hard part is no longer “show average drift is negative.” The exact remaining task is:

> Show that every positive integer valuation sequence, unless it has already descended, eventually enters a prefix whose exact bound `B` lies below a certified lower bound for a hypothetical minimal counterexample.

This formulation exposes the distinction between local 2-adic freedom and global positive-integer arithmetic.

Arbitrary finite valuation words can have positive integer shadows, so merely finding many good finite words is insufficient. A successful route must control **which bad words can persist as the actual prefixes of one fixed positive integer**.

## 6. Candidate search objects generated by L1

L1 supports three concrete machine-searchable approaches:

1. **Adaptive residue partition:** refine low binary residues until `B<=M` or until the family maps exactly into a previously handled family.
2. **Finite recursive certificate graph:** nodes encode affine residue families; edges encode exact Collatz prefixes; a well-founded rank on node/parameter pairs rules out an infinite unresolved branch.
3. **Rewrite-system interpretation:** translate the same decrease into a well-founded order on exact mixed-radix rewrite states.

A naive finite residue tree with bounded depth cannot suffice because stopping times are unbounded. The object worth searching for is therefore a **finite recursive graph with a ranking**, not a finite-depth cover.
