# Historical Round 6B source recovery

The dossier and Python checker in this directory are exact recoveries: their SHA256 values match the separately saved original checksum manifest. They are archived under the owner's authorization to publish the mathematical work.

The documents retain their historical wording and status. They are not new Lean proofs, have not been newly audited, and do not solve or disprove Collatz. The Python checker was not rerun during this recovery.

See `provenance.json` for source identifiers and hashes, and the parent catalog for the unresolved archive-download coverage gap. The original checksum manifest also lists files that were not recovered into this directory; their presence in that manifest does not imply they are included here.
