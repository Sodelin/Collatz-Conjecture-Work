# Prior-question target record: YAH Conjecture 4.14

Recorded 7 September 2026, before a new proof-development attempt on this target. Status: **candidate for feasibility assessment; no qualifying new result established**. This record is not a submission and does not imply that this problem is tractable.

## Prior source

Emre Yolcu, Scott Aaronson and Marijn J. H. Heule, *An Automated Approach to the Collatz Conjecture*, [arXiv 2105.14697, version 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.14697v3), Conjecture 4.14 and Proposition 4.15. The original paper predates this project's 2026 artifacts. The exact historical statement is in the named conjecture, unlike the scalar restriction chosen within this project.

The target is the assertion that every nonconvergent ordinary positive Collatz orbit visits an integer congruent to 5 or 7 modulo 8. Proposition 4.15 relates this to convergence of a particular partial map. The paper states the conjecture open. A bounded current search located no resolution; that is not a proof of present-day open status. Refresh the status against primary literature before investing in a proof attempt.

## Success condition and baseline

The full success condition is a proof of that orbit statement, or a rigorous counterexample meeting all its quantifiers. A partial result needs an independently meaningful theorem with an explicit demonstrated implication toward this previously stated target; selecting a smaller special case internally does not automatically meet the catalog's standard.

Before claiming a bound or covered class is progress, establish the literature's previous bound or class and the strict improvement. Finite numerical checks, a search timeout, an equivalent reformulation and successful certificate replay alone are not the required theorem.

## Relationship to existing work

The existing dimension-one arctic-natural first-step obstruction excludes specified proof mechanisms. It does not establish convergence of the partial map, force visits to the required residues or resolve Conjecture 4.14. Its certificates may be useful methodology, but no transfer theorem to this target has been proved.

The alternative suggestion from the curator is the general matrix-interpretation question in YAH section 6. That direction requires an exact interpretation class, carriers, orders, admissibility conditions and allowed transformations before an all-dimensional assertion can be meaningful. A finite dimension or coefficient search cannot establish universal impossibility.

YAH section 4.3 already proves termination of all eleven single-rule-deleted systems. A valid first full rule-removal step for the original system would therefore complete a Collatz proof using the known remainder results. A positive dimension-two first step is not a modest intermediate target.
