# Verification map

The [generated verification matrix](../_generated/VERIFICATION_MATRIX.md)
shows which claims are fully checked within scope, merely tested by a bounded
artifact, or formally proved. The complete commands and caveats remain in the
[verification manifest](../../verification/README.md) and [Lean verification
policy](../../lean/VERIFICATION_POLICY.md).
