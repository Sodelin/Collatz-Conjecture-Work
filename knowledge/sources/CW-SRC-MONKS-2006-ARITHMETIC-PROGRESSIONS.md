# The Sufficiency of Arithmetic Progressions for the 3x+1 Conjecture

[Source index](INDEX.md)

## Citation and identifiers

Kenneth M. Monks, *Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society* 134.10 (2006), 2861–2872. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-06-08567-4) · [Open Zotero item](zotero://select/library/items/SPMZEJYQ)

## Role in this project

Shows every nonconstant arithmetic progression is sufficient. This stronger prior art lowers the standalone novelty of the project's three-terminal trajectory normal form and its `7 mod 8` component.

## Audit boundary

Sufficiency means that proving convergence on the set would suffice; it does not prove convergence on that set. The project's normal form remains an elementary reduction, not a Collatz proof.
