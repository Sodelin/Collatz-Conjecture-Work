## Role in this project

Diophantine background for finiteness arguments involving fixed prime support and exponential equations that can arise in cycle or endpoint constraints.

## Audit boundary

No direct application closing a Collatz route is currently accepted. Effectivity, uniformity, and the translation from project variables must be proved rather than inferred from general finiteness.
