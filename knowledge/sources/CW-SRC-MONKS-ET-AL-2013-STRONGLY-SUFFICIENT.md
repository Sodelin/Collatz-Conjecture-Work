# Strongly Sufficient Sets and the Distribution of Arithmetic Sequences in the 3x+1 Graph

[Source index](INDEX.md)

## Citation and identifiers

Keenan Monks, Kenneth G. Monks, Kenneth M. Monks, and Maria Monks, *Discrete Mathematics* 313 (2013), 468–489. [arXiv:1204.3904](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.3904)

[Open Zotero item](zotero://select/library/items/KGV28XFI)

## Role in this project

Stronger sufficient-set and union framework used to assess whether trajectory-normal-form terminal families add anything beyond known graph structure.

## Audit boundary

The framework does not establish the Collatz conjecture without convergence on an appropriate sufficient set. Project reductions must not be described as new global progress solely because their terminal sets are sufficient.
