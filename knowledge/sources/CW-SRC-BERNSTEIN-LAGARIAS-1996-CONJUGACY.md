# The 3x+1 Conjugacy Map

[Source index](INDEX.md)

## Citation and identifiers

Daniel J. Bernstein and Jeffrey C. Lagarias, *Canadian Journal of Mathematics* 48.6 (1996), 1154–1169. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1996-060-x) · [Open Zotero item](zotero://select/library/items/5E72C5L9)

## Role in this project

Classical authority for 2-adic parity coding, the conjugacy map, and full-shift background. It is essential to diagnosing when finite compatible parity data produces only a 2-adic ghost rather than an ordinary positive seed.

## Audit boundary

2-adic realization is not positive-integer realization. The project must prove ordinary positivity/integrality or stabilization separately; the conjugacy alone does not supply a Collatz counterexample.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Match parity-bit order, map normalization, and conjugacy direction.
- [ ] Separate 2-adic continuity from Archimedean or positive-integer claims.
- [ ] Audit every infinite-prefix construction for the ghost error.
