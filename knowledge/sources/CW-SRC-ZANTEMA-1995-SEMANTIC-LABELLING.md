# Termination of Term Rewriting by Semantic Labelling

[Source index](INDEX.md)

## Citation and identifiers

Hans Zantema, *Fundamenta Informaticae* 24 (1995), 89–105. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3233/FI-1995-24124)

[Open Zotero item](zotero://select/library/items/56D9ZUID)

## Role in this project

Foundational semantic-labelling framework needed to state precisely what the fixed two-state YAH algebra does and does not exclude.

## Audit boundary

The project's finite cancellation certificates concern one fixed labelled algebra and selected additive interpretation classes. They are not a general refutation of semantic labelling.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Reconstruct the model and labelling hypotheses used by the project.
- [ ] Check that “semantic” and merely syntactic labelled no-go claims are separated.
- [ ] Obtain a term-rewriting specialist review.
