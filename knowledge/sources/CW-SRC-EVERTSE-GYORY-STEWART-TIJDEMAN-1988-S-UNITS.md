## Role in this project

Survey/application background for assessing whether fixed-prime-support Collatz equations reduce to known S-unit finiteness results.

## Audit boundary

General S-unit finiteness does not automatically provide a uniform Collatz bound, an algorithm, or a fixed-seed global argument. The precise reduction remains a separate obligation.
