# On Unsettleable Arithmetical Problems

[Source index](INDEX.md)

## Citation and identifiers

John H. Conway, *The American Mathematical Monthly* 120.3 (2013), 192–198. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4169/amer.math.monthly.120.03.192) · [Accessible PDF](https://gwern.net/doc/cs/computable/2013-conway.pdf)

[Open Zotero item](zotero://select/library/items/8TUR277M)

## Role in this project

Methodological boundary showing that simple-looking generalized arithmetic iteration systems can encode undecidable behavior. It cautions against assuming that a finite rewrite representation guarantees an easy certificate.

## Audit boundary

Undecidability of a generalized system is not undecidability of the classical Collatz conjecture and supplies neither proof nor disproof.
