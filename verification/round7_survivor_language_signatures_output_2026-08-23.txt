Interpretation
--------------
- At one-bit horizon there are only the expected low-only, high-only, and both-child survivor patterns.
- At horizon two, the observed signature count remains small in several later levels (7 signatures at K=12 and K=13), but it is not monotone and briefly reaches 11 at K=11.
- At horizons three and four, distinct signatures grow substantially (31 at K=12 for h=3; 57 at K=11 for h=4).
- Therefore the raw bounded-search survivor bitmask is NOT presently evidence for a tiny finite automaton. It is too coarse an observable and its apparent state complexity grows with horizon.
- This does not refute Route AB. The next quotient should use exact mixed-radix/carry state from the Yolcu-Aaronson-Heule representation rather than survivor bitmasks alone.
