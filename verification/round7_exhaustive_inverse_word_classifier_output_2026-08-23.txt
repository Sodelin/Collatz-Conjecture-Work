There is no arbitrary inverse-depth parameter in this classifier. For a forward state at time t with s odd branches:

- a strict-slope winner has e<t-s even inverses and total length at most t-1, as proved in L5;
- the only remaining slope boundary has r=s odd inverses, e=t-s even inverses, total length t, leading coefficient exactly 2^K, and succeeds exactly when its intercept is smaller than R (with the positivity threshold checked separately).

The corrected search exhausts both cases.

The final validator recomputes the exact uniform forward affine state and odd
count, replays every inverse E/O operation with its divisibility guard,
compares the resulting affine pair exactly, and verifies the exact threshold.
Concrete samples remain only as a redundant implementation guardrail.

A `class_miss` therefore means no certificate exists in THIS WHOLE-FAMILY INVERSE-WORD CLASS for the tested cylinder. It does not imply divergence and is not evidence against Collatz.

Exact equal-slope boundary regression
-------------------------------------
For every integer x>=0,

  T^3(8*x+5) = T^3(8*x+4) = 3*x+2,

and the inverse word from the common target is OEE. Thus

  0 < 8*x+4 < 8*x+5

despite the two affine families having the same leading coefficient 8. This certificate is redundant with a shorter strict-slope certificate for the same cylinder, but it is an exact regression for the boundary case omitted by the original classifier.
