IMPORTANT
---------
There is no arbitrary inverse-depth parameter in this classifier. For a forward state at time t with s odd branches, L5 proves that every successful inverse word has fewer than t-s even inverses and total length at most t-1. The search exhausts that finite class.

A `class_miss` therefore means no certificate exists in THIS WHOLE-FAMILY INVERSE-WORD CLASS for the tested cylinder. It does not imply divergence and is not evidence against Collatz.

Interpretation
--------------
- The whole-family inverse-word class is now mathematically bounded and exhaustible at each fixed cylinder.
- Persistent misses must be attacked by a genuinely stronger certificate semantics, not by saying “search this same class deeper.”
- Candidate stronger mechanisms include parameter refinement during a macro, recursive state-graph transitions, or a ranked mixed-radix automaton.
- This is exactly the distinction between SEARCH_DEPTH_MISS and CLASS_MISS required by the shared Proof-Attack framework.
